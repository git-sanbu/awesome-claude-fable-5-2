It had **66 GitHub stars in the 2026-11-11 search snapshot**; its content and star count may change.

## What to look for

- How a visual brief describes composition, color, typography and interaction.
- The relationship between a published prompt, screenshot and implementation.
- Whether the example has responsive behavior, accessible controls and a license suitable for your intended use.

Visit the [original repository](https://github.com/MiaAI-Lab/Claude-Fable-5.2-HTML-Files) for its files and current terms. Model attribution and results are the maintainer's claims; we have not independently run these studies or established how much manual work was involved.
