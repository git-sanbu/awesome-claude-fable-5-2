# Claude Fable 5.2 examples shared on X

[Home](../README.md) · [Resource directory](README.md) · [GitHub projects](projects.md) · [Best practices](best-practices.md) · [Official resources](official-resources.md)

These examples were collected on 2026-11-11 from the [awesome-fable5-2-videos](https://github.com/yihui-dev/awesome-fable5-2-videos) prompt catalogue, supplemented by a web search for further X posts; the two entries at the end of the table came from that search and are not listed in the catalogue. Entries are ordered most notable first, a collection-time judgment rather than a measured ranking. The original posts' engagement counts and the demos themselves were not independently accessible or verified during this research; descriptions and prompts below are attributed to the catalogue and creators, and model use, workflow, and results have not been reproduced. No claim of viral ranking is made.
