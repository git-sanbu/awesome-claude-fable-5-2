# Community conduct

Help keep this collection welcoming and useful. Be respectful, discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people, credit creators, and respect privacy and intellectual property. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, spam and abusive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, close or remove contributions that violate these standards. To report a concern, use a GitHub issue only if it is safe to discuss publicly; for sensitive matters, use GitHub's reporting tools or contact a maintainer privately through a channel they have made available. Do not publish private information in an issue.
